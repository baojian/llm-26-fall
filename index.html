Course overview课程概览

This course covers the foundations and modern frontiers of Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a heavy emphasis on Large Language Models (LLMs). You will learn the modern pipeline for building an LLM: tokenization, language modeling, the Transformer, pretraining, evaluation, post-training, retrieval, alignment, efficiency, and agents. 本课程讲授自然语言处理（NLP）的基础与前沿，重点是大语言模型（LLMs）。你将学习构建一个 LLM 的完整现代流程：分词、语言建模、Transformer、预训练、评测、后训练、检索、对齐、效率与智能体。

This edition's storyline: one denoiser, many corruptions本学期的主线：一个去噪器，多种加噪方式

A language model is a learned reverse process. Pick a way to corrupt a token sequence (the forward process), train a network to undo one step of it (the denoiser), and run the reverse process from pure corruption back to text (the sampler). Every model in this course is that triple with a different corruption. The NLP content is the same as before; the story that connects it is new. 语言模型是一个学到的反向过程。选定一种破坏 token 序列的方式（前向过程），训练一个网络去撤销其中一步（去噪器），再从完全被破坏的状态出发运行反向过程，直到还原出文本（采样器）。本课程中的每个模型都是这样一个三元组，只是加噪方式不同。NLP 的内容与以往相同，串起它们的故事是新的。

Three facts carry the argument, and each gets its own in-class demonstration: BERT's loss is one step of absorbing-state diffusion (Week 8); a left-to-right LM is masked diffusion with a fixed unmasking order (Week 8); and post-training is the same recipe for both families, with SFT as denoising on curated data and RLHF/GRPO/DPO as reward-tilted reverse processes (Week 10). You will build one small code base with three functions, corrupt(x, t), denoiser(x_t, t, cond), and sample(denoiser, cond). Week 5's Transformer is the denoiser. Every later week changes corrupt or sample, never the network. 三个事实支撑这条主线，每个都有对应的课堂演示：BERT 的损失就是吸收态扩散的一步（第 8 周）；从左到右的语言模型就是去掩码顺序固定的掩码扩散（第 8 周）；两类模型的后训练是同一套配方——SFT 是在精选数据上做去噪，RLHF/GRPO/DPO 则是被奖励“倾斜”的反向过程（第 10 周）。你将只写一个小代码库，包含三个函数：corrupt(x, t)、denoiser(x_t, t, cond) 与 sample(denoiser, cond)。第 5 周的 Transformer 就是去噪器；之后每一周只改 corrupt 或 sample，从不改网络。

Course project课程项目

One project per student, reviewed inside a pod of three (one master's student and two undergraduates), with a short oral defence. What we assess is judgement: choosing the question, designing the evaluation, and deciding what to keep from what your coding assistant proposed.每人一个项目，在三人小组（一名硕士生加两名本科生）内互评，并有简短的口头答辩。我们考察的是判断力：如何选题、如何设计评测，以及在编程助手的建议中留下什么。

Track A · Build方向 A · 构建

Build a small chat model under a fixed budget: tokenizer → pretrain → SFT → RL → evaluation, following nanochat's stages. Either an autoregressive model or a masked diffusion LM.在固定预算下构建一个小型对话模型：分词器 → 预训练 → SFT → RL → 评测，按 nanochat 的阶段进行。可选自回归模型或掩码扩散语言模型。

Track B · Reproduce and extend方向 B · 复现并扩展

Reproduce one main table from a 2025–26 paper and extend it with an ablation, a new dataset, or a new setting.复现一篇 2025–26 年论文的一张主表，并通过消融、新数据集或新设定加以扩展。

Every project must include something an assistant cannot supply每个项目都必须包含助手给不了的东西

Choose at least one: a dataset you collected yourself; a Chinese-language angle; a reproduction of a table from a 2025–26 paper with a measured discrepancy; or a negative result, explained.至少选一项：你自己收集的数据集；一个中文视角；复现一篇 2025–26 年论文中的一张表并测出差异；或一个被解释清楚的负面结果。

Deliverables交付物

  1. Pods formed (Week 5).组建小组（第 5 周）。
  2. One-page proposal (Week 7): problem, dataset, baselines.一页提案（第 7 周）：问题、数据集、基线。
  3. Baseline reproduction (Week 9).基线复现（第 9 周）。
  4. Pod draft review (Week 10): each pod reviews its members' drafts and writes one shared comparison page relating its three projects.小组草稿互评（第 10 周）：小组内互评成员草稿，并合写一页“比较页”，把三个项目联系起来。
  5. Poster session (Week 12): preliminary results, pods present side by side, structured peer review.海报展示（第 12 周）：初步结果，小组并排展示，结构化互评。
  6. Oral defence (Weeks 15–16): 8 minutes per student during exercise time, with the instructor and TAs.口头答辩（第 15–16 周）：练习时间内每人 8 分钟，由教师与助教主持。
  7. Final report (Week 17): at most 4 pages of main content in the ACL template, unlimited appendix, plus code and a one-page decision log: what the assistant proposed, what you rejected, and why. The log is graded.期末报告（第 17 周）：使用 ACL 模板，正文不超过 4 页，附录不限，另附代码与一页决策日志：助手提了什么、你否决了什么、为什么。决策日志计入成绩。

What good looks like怎样算好

  • Undergraduates: a careful reproduction, one extension, an honest evaluation, the decision log, and the defence.本科生：认真的复现、一个扩展、诚实的评测、决策日志与答辩。
  • Master's students: an original question with an ablation, one ★ component (a continuous-time formulation, a sampler analysis, or a diffusion-vs-AR comparison), and reviewer duty for the pod.硕士生：一个带消融实验的原创问题、一个 ★ 组件（连续时间表述、采样器分析或扩散–自回归对比），并承担小组评审。

Each student receives a fixed GPU-hour budget on the university cluster. Small models are the point of this course, not a limitation.每位同学在学校集群上有固定的 GPU 小时预算。小模型正是本课程的要点，而非限制。

Policies课程政策

AI assistantsAI 助手

Allowed for concepts, debugging, and boilerplate on assignments and the project. Every submission carries a disclosure box saying which tools you used and for what. Quizzes and in-class exercises are closed-assistant. The project includes a graded decision log of what the assistant proposed and what you rejected.允许在作业与项目中用于概念理解、调试与样板代码。每次提交都须附使用声明框，说明用了哪些工具、用在何处。小测与课堂练习禁止使用助手。项目需附决策日志（计分），记录助手的建议与你的取舍。

Academic integrity学术诚信

Please read the course policy on academic integrity. Undisclosed AI use, copied code without attribution, and fabricated results are treated as misconduct.请阅读课程的学术诚信政策。未声明的 AI 使用、未注明来源的代码抄袭以及伪造结果均按学术不端处理。
